## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | Far-right AfD wins German state election in historic breakthrough
Germany's far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) won a state election, a historic first that has shaken the country's political establishment and sent a chill through Europe. Chancellor Friedrich Merz expressed shock at the result but said he would double down on his government's unpopular reform agenda. The victory has tested Germany's long-standing political "firewall" against the far right as never before and raised questions about the AfD's path toward national power.

**Why it matters:** A far-right electoral triumph in Europe's largest economy challenges the post-war political consensus and could reshape the continent's political landscape.

### #2 | Canada's retaliatory tariffs on US goods take effect as trade war escalates
Canada's counter-tariffs on American goods — including milk, perfume, and golf clubs — took effect, deepening a prolonged trade war between the two neighbors. President Trump threatened to escalate further as Canadian officials braced for an extended dispute, while opposition from Big Tech and business groups grew louder. The tit-for-tat measures are straining one of the world's largest bilateral trading relationships.

**Why it matters:** The expanding US-Canada tariff fight threatens to disrupt integrated North American supply chains and set a precedent for US trade policy worldwide.

🏷️ Trade / North America

### #3 | Hurricane Lowell nears Hawaii, prompting warnings for multiple islands
Hurricane Lowell, a powerful Category 2 storm, closed in on Hawaii, triggering a hurricane warning for Kauai County and a tropical storm warning for Oahu, with a tornado watch posted for Kauai. Forecasters warned the storm's track could produce a rare, near-direct hit on the islands, prompting urgent preparations. The storm follows dangerous surf and flooding elsewhere as the Pacific hurricane season intensifies.

**Why it matters:** A rare direct strike by a major hurricane on the Hawaiian Islands could cause significant damage to a heavily populated chain in its path.

🏷️ Weather / US

### #4 | Trump suggests renaming New Mexico "New America," sparking backlash
President Trump posted a map suggesting the state of New Mexico be renamed "New America," triggering a torrent of online memes and sharp pushback from state Democrats. The proposal follows earlier moves to rename geographic features amid the ongoing US-Canada trade dispute. Critics called the idea a distraction, while supporters amplified it on social media.

**Why it matters:** The proposal, dismissed by many as unserious, is the latest flashpoint in a broader pattern of provocative renaming and territorial rhetoric from the White House.

🏷️ US Politics

### #5 | Documents suggest New Yorkers were misled about air quality after 9/11
Newly reviewed documents suggest that New Yorkers were misled about the safety of the air in the aftermath of the 9/11 attacks, when toxic dust from the collapsed towers settled over Lower Manhattan. The reporting revisits long-standing questions about what officials knew and when, and about the health consequences for residents and first responders exposed to the debris. The findings have renewed calls for accountability and continued health support for survivors.

**Why it matters:** The revelations reopen a painful chapter about public-health communication during a national emergency and its lasting toll on thousands of people.
