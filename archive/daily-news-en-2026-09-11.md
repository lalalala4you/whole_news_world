## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | Houthis seize key Red Sea port of Mokha, tightening grip on Bab al-Mandab
Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi movement has reportedly captured the strategic port city of Mokha on the Red Sea coast, according to multiple reports confirmed hours apart. The advance follows a broader Houthi push along Yemen's western coastline and puts the group closer to controlling traffic through the Bab al-Mandab Strait, one of the world's busiest shipping corridors. Analysts warn the move threatens Saudi oil exports and could further disrupt commercial shipping already rerouted around the Cape of Good Hope.

**Why it matters:** Control of Bab al-Mandab gives the Houthis leverage over a chokepoint carrying a significant share of global trade, raising the risk of higher energy and freight costs worldwide.

### #2 | Trump promises $5,000 payout to every American adult if Republicans win midterms
US President Donald Trump has pledged a $5,000 payment to every American adult if Republicans hold Congress in November's midterm elections, a promise he repeated at the Republican midterm convention. The offer — estimated at roughly $1.3 trillion — has drawn accusations of bribery from critics and questions from economists about its legality and affordability, given that US national debt has passed a historic $40 trillion. Analysts note the proposal echoes election-year stimulus politics while raising fresh concerns about fiscal discipline.

**Why it matters:** A fiscal commitment of this scale would reshape US budget politics and global bond markets, and it escalates the stakes of a midterm cycle already marked by partisan confrontation.

🏷️ US Politics & Economy

### #3 | Russian strikes hit Ukrainian shopping centres as Canada pledges air defence support
Ukraine says five people were killed and 67 injured in a Russian strike on a shopping centre in Pavlohrad, the third attack on a mall within days. The strikes come as Moscow shifts to new shelling tactics that officials say are deepening civilian suffering and disrupting daily life across the country. During a visit to Calgary, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy secured a Canadian offer of air defence support, hosted by Prime Minister Mark Carney.

**Why it matters:** Repeated attacks on civilian sites and Western air-defence commitments underscore how far the war remains from de-escalation, with direct consequences for European security and energy markets.

🏷️ Europe & Security

### #4 | North Korea builds new uranium facility to expand nuclear arsenal, UN agency says
The UN's nuclear watchdog says North Korea has constructed a new uranium enrichment facility, a development that would allow Pyongyang to significantly expand its nuclear weapons stockpile. The finding adds to a series of recent indications that the country is accelerating its nuclear and missile programmes despite international sanctions. Regional powers have yet to respond formally, but the disclosure is likely to feature in upcoming security consultations among the US, South Korea and Japan.

**Why it matters:** A larger enrichment capacity erodes the already fragile non-proliferation framework in Northeast Asia and complicates any future path to denuclearisation talks.

🏷️ Asia & Nuclear Proliferation

### #5 | Nepal flood disaster: rescuers find survivors nine days on as climate compensation calls grow
Rescue teams in Nepal have pulled two more people alive from a tunnel, nine days after catastrophic floods and landslides triggered by monsoon rains devastated parts of the country. At least dozens remain missing, and Nepal has demanded that major polluters "wake up" and pay climate compensation for the disaster, which followed the flooding of a Nepal–Tibet border valley. Singapore has dispatched humanitarian aid, including medical supplies and DNA test kits, to assist identification and relief efforts.

**Why it matters:** The disaster has become a flashpoint in the global debate over climate finance, with vulnerable nations pressing wealthy emitters for compensation as extreme weather intensifies.
