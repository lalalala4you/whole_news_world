## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | US and Iran Agree to Peace Talks in Doha Amid Strait of Hormuz Tensions

President Trump claimed Iran has agreed to hold peace talks in Doha on Tuesday, following recent clashes and an exchange of fire in the strategic Strait of Hormuz. The development comes after months of escalating US-Iran hostilities that included direct strikes and counterstrikes. Energy analysts warn that the conflict has already fundamentally transformed the global energy sector, accelerating the shift toward renewables and the search for alternative shipping routes. Iran remains determined to win international recognition of its control over the strait, a vital chokepoint for global oil shipments.

**Why it matters:** A breakthrough or failure in these talks could determine the trajectory of global energy prices and regional stability across the Middle East.

🏷️ Geopolitics / Energy

### #2 | Venezuela Devastated by Deadly Earthquakes as Rescue Efforts Continue

Venezuela is reeling from a series of powerful earthquakes that struck last week, causing widespread destruction and loss of life. Among the most heart-wrenching stories is the rescue of 2-year-old Kleiber Moran, pulled from the rubble six days after the quakes — his aunt told the BBC she will give him "mother's warmth." Families are still desperately searching for loved ones in hard-hit areas like Caraballeda, La Guaira, where residents are digging through debris by hand. Meanwhile, Venezuelan police officers have been arrested over alleged looting in the aftermath of the disaster.

**Why it matters:** The scale of the humanitarian crisis may overwhelm Venezuela's already fragile infrastructure, with international aid coordination urgently needed.

🏷️ Humanitarian / Latin America

### #3 | Ukrainian National Charged in Germany Over Nord Stream Pipeline Sabotage

German authorities have charged a Ukrainian national in connection with the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline explosions, a case that has serious implications for Ukraine's relationship with Germany. Ukraine has denied any involvement in the sabotage. The charges mark a major breakthrough in one of the most significant energy infrastructure attacks in modern history, which severed key gas pipelines linking Russia to Europe and reshaped the continent's energy landscape overnight.

**Why it matters:** The case could strain Ukraine-Germany relations at a critical moment in the war with Russia and reignite debates about European energy security.

🏷️ Europe / Energy Security

### #4 | Trump Blocks Long-Term Renewal of US-Canada-Mexico Trade Deal (USMCA)

The Trump administration has declined to renew the USMCA trade pact on a long-term basis, instead opting to keep the agreement alive through short annual reviews. The move marks a dramatic reversal from Trump's earlier praise of the deal, which he once championed as a signature achievement. The decision injects significant uncertainty into North American trade relations, affecting supply chains across automotive, agriculture, and manufacturing sectors that depend on cross-border commerce.

**Why it matters:** The short-leash approach creates chronic uncertainty for businesses across North America and signals a potential unraveling of continental economic integration.

🏷️ Trade / North America

### #5 | Sudan's RSF Committed Crimes Against Humanity in El Fasher, Says Amnesty International

Amnesty International has released a damning report accusing Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (RSF) of committing crimes against humanity in the city of El Fasher, including ethnic cleansing and a systematic campaign against civilians. The report adds to the growing body of evidence documenting atrocities in Sudan's ongoing civil war, which has already displaced millions and created one of the world's worst humanitarian crises. The international community has faced criticism for its limited response.

**Why it matters:** The report strengthens the case for international accountability mechanisms, including potential ICC prosecutions, as the conflict continues to spiral.
