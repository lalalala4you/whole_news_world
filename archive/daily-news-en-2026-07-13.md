## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | US-Iran Conflict Escalates as Both Sides Trade Strikes Over Strait of Hormuz
The United States and Iran exchanged a fresh wave of military strikes within hours of each other, with Iran claiming it struck US military bases in Jordan and Bahrain in retaliation for American attacks. Iran has also declared the Strait of Hormuz — a critical chokepoint for roughly 20% of global oil shipments — effectively closed, warning vessels not to transit without its authorization. President Trump rejected Iran's closure claim, but oil prices surged more than 4% on the escalating tensions. The confrontation follows the collapse of tentative US-Iran diplomatic talks, with Tehran now calling diplomacy "futile."

**Why it matters:** A prolonged disruption of the Strait of Hormuz would send shockwaves through the global economy, driving up energy prices and threatening supply chains worldwide.

### #2 | At Least 27 Killed in Devastating Bangkok Pub Fire
A fire broke out just after midnight at a pub in Bangkok's Chatuchak district, killing at least 27 people and leaving eight others critically injured. Patrons were seen running through flames to escape, and Thailand's Prime Minister Anutin, who visited the scene, noted that many had fled toward the back of the establishment where no fire escapes were available. Firefighters arrived to find chaotic and harrowing scenes. It is one of the deadliest such incidents in the popular tourist destination in recent years.

**Why it matters:** The tragedy raises urgent questions about fire safety enforcement in Thailand's nightlife venues, a sector that underpins the country's vital tourism industry.

### #3 | US Senator Lindsey Graham, Key Trump Ally, Dies Suddenly at 71
Senator Lindsey Graham of South Carolina, one of Donald Trump's closest congressional allies, died following what his office described as a "brief and sudden illness." Initial findings and later reports indicate the cause of death was an aortic dissection, a serious heart condition. Graham, 71, had been a towering figure in Republican foreign policy circles and was widely seen as an indispensable bridge between the Trump White House and Capitol Hill. His death sent shockwaves through Washington, with tributes pouring in from both domestic and foreign leaders, including Israel and Ukraine.

**Why it matters:** Graham's passing removes a pivotal Trump loyalist from the Senate at a moment when the administration is navigating multiple international crises, potentially reshaping the balance of power on Capitol Hill.

🏷️ US Politics / Obituary

### #4 | China Evacuates Nearly Two Million as Powerful Typhoon Bavi Makes Landfall
China evacuated close to two million residents as Typhoon Bavi slammed into Zhejiang province, bringing destructive winds and torrential rainfall. Schools, workplaces, and outdoor activities have been suspended across the region, and numerous transport services — including flights and high-speed rail — have been cancelled. Authorities have warned that the typhoon season in China is expected to be unusually active this year, with more storms likely to follow in Bavi's wake. Coastal communities remain on high alert for flooding and landslides.

**Why it matters:** A mass evacuation on this scale tests China's disaster-response infrastructure during an already turbulent year, with economic and humanitarian stakes high across one of the country's most densely populated and industrialized coastal zones.

🏷️ Asia / Natural Disaster / China

### #5 | Zelenskyy Replaces Ukraine's Prime Minister, Allies Pledge More Air Defence Aid
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy dismissed his prime minister and signalled a broader overhaul of law enforcement leadership, in the most significant government reshuffle since the start of Russia's full-scale invasion. The moves come as Ukraine's battlefield momentum shows signs of shifting, with Kyiv's forces conducting drone strikes deep enough to force Russia to suspend commercial shipping in the Sea of Azov. Meanwhile, Western allies have agreed to muster additional air defence systems for Ukraine, aiming to bolster the country's ability to defend its cities and critical infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** Leadership changes and renewed allied support signal a potential inflection point in the war, as both Kyiv and Moscow seek to reshape the strategic landscape heading into the second half of 2026.
