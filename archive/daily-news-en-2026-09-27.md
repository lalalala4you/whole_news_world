## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | Trump rejects Iran's proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz
US President Donald Trump has rejected an Iranian proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and bring an end to the current fighting, according to multiple reports. Iran's foreign minister acknowledged Trump's comments but said Tehran is still awaiting the "definitive views" of mediators, while insisting the country remains committed to a diplomatic solution. Analysts cited in the coverage suggest Washington expects renewed strikes after the US midterm elections. The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most important oil chokepoint, so any disruption there carries immediate consequences for global energy markets.

**Why it matters:** A closed or contested Strait of Hormuz threatens global oil supply and could sharply escalate tensions across the Middle East.

🏷️ Middle East / Geopolitics

### #2 | Xi and Trump meet in Washington — and Beijing claims the win
Chinese President Xi Jinping met President Trump in Washington, where Xi laid out terms aimed at avoiding a US-China military conflict. Commentary in The Straits Times and The Guardian described the summit as a win for Beijing, noting that Trump hailed the visit as "great" even though almost nothing of substance was achieved on the thorny issues dividing the two powers. The encounter underscored how much harder a more assertive China has proven for Washington to squeeze than the China of 2018.

**Why it matters:** The tone and outcome of US-China diplomacy shapes trade, technology, and security policy well beyond the two countries.

🏷️ US-China / Diplomacy

### #3 | Ethiopia: rebel offensive raises fears of renewed civil war
Fighting has escalated in Ethiopia after Tigrayan rebels formed a new coalition aimed at overthrowing the government of Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. The escalation has revived fears of a return to full-scale civil war in a country still recovering from the devastating 2020–2022 conflict. Regional observers warn that a renewed war would deepen an already severe humanitarian crisis in the Horn of Africa.

**Why it matters:** Instability in Ethiopia risks destabilising the wider Horn of Africa and compounding a major humanitarian emergency.

🏷️ Africa / Conflict

### #4 | Pakistan locks down Islamabad as Imran Khan's party vows to march
Pakistani authorities deployed tens of thousands of police and barricaded roads in a lockdown of the capital, Islamabad, ahead of a promised march by Imran Khan's Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) party. PTI is demanding the release of the jailed former prime minister. In a related move, police detained Khan's sisters ahead of the protest. The standoff marks another flashpoint in the prolonged confrontation between Khan's supporters and the current government.

**Why it matters:** A political showdown in nuclear-armed Pakistan carries real risks for stability in an already volatile South Asian region.

🏷️ South Asia / Politics

### #5 | Singapore haze worsens as PSI hits unhealthy range
Air quality in Singapore deteriorated sharply, with the Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) reaching unhealthy levels in the central, eastern and western regions. The worsening haze forced organisers to shorten Decathlon's Kiprun half-marathon to 10km. The episode comes amid unusually hot conditions, after a joint-highest temperature for the month — 35.8°C — was recorded at Woodlands on September 26. Haze remains a recurring transboundary issue for Singapore and its neighbours.

**Why it matters:** Unhealthy air quality poses public-health risks and is a recurring regional challenge linked to seasonal land-clearing fires.

🏷️ Singapore / Environment
