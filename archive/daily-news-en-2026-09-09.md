### #1 | US strikes five Iranian oil tankers as Tehran retaliates; crude heads toward $100
The United States struck five Iranian oil tankers — sinking at least one — saying the vessels were involved in attempted strikes on a US warship in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran responded with a missile barrage targeting a US base in Jordan and American ships near the strategic waterway. The escalation pushed crude prices toward $100 a barrel as the conflict between Washington and Tehran intensified sharply.

**Why it matters:** A direct exchange of fire over oil shipping in the Strait of Hormuz threatens global energy supplies and risks a wider regional war at a moment of already-severe instability.

### #2 | Trump bans some Canadian imports as North America trade war escalates
President Trump signed orders banning some Canadian products from the US market — including dairy, most alcohol, and motorcycles — as the North American trade war deepened. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney doubled down on his country's pivot away from the United States, while Canada's own retaliatory tariffs took effect. The tit-for-tat measures mark a sharp deterioration in relations between the two neighbors.

**Why it matters:** The expanding trade war between the US and Canada is disrupting deeply integrated North American supply chains and reshaping a core US alliance.

🏷️ Trade / North America

### #3 | John E. Sununu wins Republican Senate primary in New Hampshire
Former senator John E. Sununu won the Republican Senate primary in New Hampshire, setting up what Democrats warn will be a tough general-election race. The result emerged from a crowded field as voters expressed a mix of disgust and apathy toward the candidates. Democrats were bracing for a hard-fought contest in a state seen as pivotal to control of the Senate.

**Why it matters:** The New Hampshire race is a key battleground that could help decide the balance of power in Washington.

### #4 | Smithsonian Secretary Lonnie Bunch to leave amid tensions with Trump
Smithsonian Secretary Lonnie Bunch, the first Black person to lead the institution, announced he will step down after months of tension with the Trump administration over efforts to reshape the Smithsonian. His departure raises questions about the institution's independence and the future of its leadership. The move comes as the administration pushes for greater control over the Smithsonian's direction.

**Why it matters:** The departure of a respected cultural leader highlights a broader struggle over the independence of US cultural and educational institutions.

🏷️ US / Culture

The case, which drew national attention, left Clancy's fate unresolved and the matter in the court of public opinion. Jurors described an "emotional rollercoaster" and frustration with the holdout.

**Why it matters:** The high-profile mistrial leaves a closely watched case without resolution and reignites debate over the issues at its heart.
