## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | Middle East marks three years since 7 October as regional tensions flare
Memorial events were held in Israel on the third anniversary of the Hamas-led attack that triggered the devastating Gaza war, with survivors and families demanding accountability over the failures that day. In Gaza, three years of war have killed tens of thousands, destroyed homes and collapsed basic services, leaving residents describing a life of "neither war nor peace." Separately, Saudi Arabia said three people died at its airports after Houthi forces claimed cross-border attacks, underscoring how the conflict continues to ripple across the region.

**Why it matters:** The anniversary highlights a war with no clear end, while fresh Houthi strikes show the risk of wider regional escalation.

### #2 | Russia-Ukraine war escalates on both fronts
A Ukrainian naval drone struck an oil tanker off the Russian resort city of Sochi, triggering a massive blaze, in one of the deepest strikes yet on Russian energy infrastructure in the Black Sea. In Ukraine, a Russian missile hit a residential block of flats while families slept, killing at least 19 people, including children. The twin attacks came as Western allies weighed further support and sanctions.

**Why it matters:** Strikes on energy and civilian targets signal a war entering a more dangerous, higher-cost phase with global energy and security implications.

🏷️ Russia–Ukraine

### #3 | Botched execution reignites US death penalty debate
Christa Pike survived a failed lethal injection in Tennessee and remains "shackled" to a hospital bed, with her lawyers saying she is angry and confused about why she is still alive. Texas is now set to carry out the first US execution since the botched procedure, reigniting a national debate over capital punishment and lethal-injection protocols. State officials have not said whether Tennessee will attempt to execute Pike again.

**Why it matters:** A rare botched execution puts the death penalty and states' execution methods back under national and legal scrutiny.

🏷️ United States

### #4 | Markets wobble as inflation fears and Middle East conflict drive tightening
Wall Street closed lower, slipping from record highs, as climbing Treasury yields pressured equities amid renewed inflation anxiety. India's central bank raised rates for the first time since 2023, reflecting a global shift toward tighter monetary policy as central banks battle inflation fuelled by the Middle East conflict. Investors are recalibrating expectations for rate cuts in the months ahead.

**Why it matters:** Rising yields and rate hikes show how the Middle East conflict is feeding global inflation and reshaping monetary policy worldwide.

### #5 | Toxic haze blankets Singapore as Indonesia's fire season worsens
Singapore was shrouded in haze, with PSI readings in the unhealthy range across the island as one of Indonesia's worst wildfire seasons in years sent smoke drifting across Southeast Asia. PM2.5 levels stayed in the normal band in the northern, southern and eastern regions, but iconic landmarks faded from view. The episode echoes the recurring transboundary haze crises that have strained regional diplomacy.

**Why it matters:** The haze underscores Southeast Asia's vulnerability to cross-border environmental crises and the limits of regional cooperation.
