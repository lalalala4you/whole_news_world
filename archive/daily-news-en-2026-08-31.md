## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | US and Iran trade strikes in first major military escalation in a month
Iran attacked US bases in Jordan after American forces struck Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz, marking the first major military escalation between the two countries in about a month. The US said its strikes were aimed at preventing Iran from laying mines in the strategic Hormuz waterway, through which much of the world's oil passes. Tehran dismissed reports that its key Kharg energy hub had been hit, while both sides continued exchanging threats.

**Why it matters:** A renewed exchange of fire around the Strait of Hormuz risks disrupting global energy supplies and escalating a volatile regional conflict at a moment of already-heightened tension.

### #2 | Nepal rescue teams race to reach hundreds trapped in hydropower tunnels after deadly floods
Rescuers in Nepal are working to reach an estimated 900 people believed trapped in hydropower tunnels after devastating floods and landslides along the Nepal–Tibet border. The disaster followed a glacial collapse in the Himalayas that sent a wall of water through the Linda River basin, destroying bridges and roads. Pope Leo has urged aid for Nepal and called for the release of hostages in Haiti as the region grapples with the aftermath.

**Why it matters:** The scale of the Himalayan flood disaster — with hundreds unaccounted for across two countries — has become a major international humanitarian emergency.

### #3 | One dead, about 15 missing after Grand Canyon flash flooding
Flash flooding tore through the Grand Canyon, leaving at least one person dead and roughly 15 more potentially missing. Authorities said all Grand Canyon hotels would close with no reopening date set as search-and-rescue operations continued near Crystal Rapids. The sudden flood overwhelmed a popular tourist corridor with little warning.

**Why it matters:** A deadly flash flood in one of America's most visited national parks underscores the growing danger of extreme-weather events in popular destinations.

🏷️ US / Extreme Weather

### #4 | US–Venezuela oil deal won't lower gas prices, experts say
The Trump administration brokered a deal with Venezuela, which sits atop the world's largest oil reserves, but analysts caution it is unlikely to lower prices at the pump for American drivers. The agreement, which relies on a powerful and divisive partner in Caracas, has drawn scrutiny over how much of a windfall the US can realistically capture. Venezuela has vowed it will not simply "hand over" its oil.

**Why it matters:** The deal reshapes US energy policy toward a sanctioned oil giant, with implications for global markets and US foreign policy — but limited immediate relief for consumers.

🏷️ Energy / Trade

### #5 | G20 finance chiefs gather in Asheville as US rallies allies on Iran amid tariff strain
US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent headed to the G20 finance meetings in Asheville, North Carolina, seeking to rally allies on Iran even as US tariffs continue to strain relations with key partners. The gathering, which drew global leaders and protesters, comes as the Iran conflict grips the global economy and bond markets face turmoil. Bessent faces a credibility test selling Washington's "American Growth Agenda" to skeptical counterparts.

**Why it matters:** The world's top economic officials are meeting at a moment of converging crises — war, tariffs, and market instability — that will test whether the US can hold together a common front.
