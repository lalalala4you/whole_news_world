## 🌍 Top 5 Global Stories

### #1 | Russian strikes kill two in Ukraine a day after deadly mall attack
Russian strikes killed at least two people in Ukraine, officials said, a day after a double-tap strike on a shopping mall in Kryvyi Rih — the hometown of President Volodymyr Zelenskyy — killed at least 16 people. EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas called the mall attack "terror by design" and threatened the harshest sanctions yet on Moscow. The escalating attacks mark a sharp intensification of civilian targeting in the long-running war.

**Why it matters:** A renewed wave of strikes on Ukrainian cities is pushing the European Union toward its most severe economic penalties on Russia yet, raising the stakes of the continent's largest conflict.

🏷️ War in Europe

### #2 | UK, Canada and Australia condemn Israel for refusing probe into Gaza aid worker killings
Britain, Canada and Australia jointly condemned Israel for refusing a criminal investigation into the 2024 killing of seven World Central Kitchen aid workers in Gaza, calling the decision "shameful." The criticism comes as Israel also re-established a previously closed West Bank settlement and admitted its soldiers fired at the car in which Palestinian child Hind Rajab was killed. Israel's government has defied mounting international protests over settlement expansion and conduct in Gaza.

**Why it matters:** The coordinated rebuke from three close Western allies deepens Israel's diplomatic isolation and signals a hardening of international pressure over its conduct in Gaza and the West Bank.

🏷️ Middle East / Diplomacy

### #3 | Trump threatens Iran's trade partners and calls Strait of Hormuz "American territory"
US President Donald Trump threatened Iran's trading partners as military strikes give way to economic pressure, and declared he views the strategic Strait of Hormuz as "American territory." Washington and Tehran have maintained hostile rhetoric ahead of a fresh round of sanctions. The standoff is straining the US-Iran relationship and unsettling global energy markets reliant on the vital shipping chokepoint.

**Why it matters:** A confrontation over the Strait of Hormuz — through which a large share of the world's oil flows — risks disrupting global energy supplies and escalating a volatile regional conflict.

🏷️ Geopolitics / Energy

### #4 | North Korea fires 10 missiles a day after Trump curtails South Korea drills
North Korea fired 10 missiles in a show of force, just one day after US President Trump ordered a scaling back of joint military drills with South Korea. The move has revived doubts in Seoul about Washington's security guarantees and rattled the region. Pyongyang's latest launch underscores the fragile security environment on the Korean Peninsula.

**Why it matters:** North Korea's provocations, combined with a reduced US-South Korea military posture, raise fresh questions about the durability of the US alliance and regional stability in Northeast Asia.

🏷️ Asia-Pacific Security

### #5 | US-Canada trade talks go down to the wire as Trump pauses tariff threat
Negotiators worked against a Friday deadline to finalize a trade deal between the US and Canada, with a Canadian official saying "we have more work to do." Trump paused his threat of tariffs on Canada and hinted at a revival of the Keystone XL pipeline, while Vice President JD Vance was reported to have mocked Canadian leader Mark Carney for trying to "out-tough" Trump on trade. The outcome will shape the world's largest bilateral trading relationship.

**Why it matters:** A trade war — or its resolution — between the two highly integrated North American economies would ripple through global supply chains and set a precedent for US trade policy worldwide.
